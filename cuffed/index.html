How it works

Three steps to cuffs.

Your coin still launches on pump.fun. The dev buy just gets locked instead of sitting free in your wallet.

01

Launch on pump.fun

Make your coin and do your dev buy like you normally would.

02

Lock the dev bag

Right after launch, your whole dev buy goes into a Streamflow lock that can't be cancelled. Pick how long, and whether it frees up a little each day or all at the end.

03

Holders can check

We verify the lock on-chain before the coin shows up as "Dev locked" on the board, with a link to the lock on Streamflow.

You approve twice: once to launch on pump.fun, once to cuff it (lock + fee split).
