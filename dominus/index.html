How it works

Trade the coin. The fees buy the hats.

No staking, no claiming, no forms. Holding is the entry.

I

Fees fill the vault

Every trade of $DOMINUS pays a creator fee. 100% of it goes to buying Limiteds and Robux, priced by RAP so the value is public.

II

The clock strikes

Each hour, one holder is picked at random. Your entries scale with your bag, so bigger holders get more chances.

III

A holder gets crowned

The winner links their Roblox user and receives the item by trade. Once a day, a top-tier Limited goes out in the Grand Drop.

FAQ

Questions from the court

How do I enter?

Buy and hold $DOMINUS. Every wallet holding the coin at the moment the clock strikes is in the draw. There is nothing to sign up for.

How do I get my item?

When you win, link your Roblox username to your wallet. The item is sent to you by trade, or as Robux for Robux drops.

How are items valued?

By RAP, the recent average price Roblox traders already use. Every item in the vault shows its RAP so anyone can check it.

What is the Grand Drop?

Once a day the biggest Limited in the vault goes to one holder. Same rules as the hourly drop, bigger hat.

Is this affiliated with Roblox?

No. $DOMINUS is an independent community memecoin and is not made, endorsed or sponsored by Roblox Corporation.
