FAQ

Do I need tokens on every chain?

No. You pay once in SOL on Solana. Prism covers the gas to deploy on every chain you pick.

Is it the same coin on every chain?

Same name, ticker and picture, with Solana as home base. Each chain gets its own pool so people trade where their wallet already is.

What does it cost?

0.02 SOL for the Solana launch plus 0.03 SOL for each extra chain. All 10 chains costs 0.29 SOL.

Where do the fees go?

Launch fees buy $PRISM on the open market and burn it. Every burn shows up in the burn pit.

Can I add a chain later?

Yes. Open your coin and add any chain you skipped. You only pay for the new chain.
