Docs

Questions

How does pairing work?+
Your coin's pool trades against the asset you pick instead of only SOL. Pair with a tokenized stock like AAPL and buyers swap that asset in and out of your coin.
Is it the same coin on every chain?+
Yes. Every chain's contract shares one total supply. Moving tokens between chains burns them on one side and mints them on the other, so the total never changes.
Which chains and assets are supported?+
Chains: Solana, Ethereum, Base, BNB Chain, Arbitrum, Avalanche, Polygon, Sui, TON and Tron. Assets: SOL, majors, tokenized stocks, ETFs and commodities. More are coming.
What does it cost?+
Each chain's network cost plus a flat platform fee, shown before you pay. Ethereum costs the most, so you can leave it off to save.
Where do trading fees go?+
Half buys back and burns $OMNI, 30% goes to the coin's creator in SOL, and 20% funds the platform.
