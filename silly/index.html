The history

How a puppy's wink became a meme

Nobody has ever pinned down who took the original photo. It surfaced as an anonymous pet pic and the internet adopted it. What we can trace is the family tree it grew out of, and how it went from camera roll to reaction image.

  1. 1993
    The ancestor

    Dogs move online

    A New Yorker cartoon gives the web its first famous dog joke, "On the Internet, nobody knows you're a dog." Dogs have been the internet's mascot ever since.

  2. 2012
    Dogs get personalities

    The guilty-dog era

    Dog shaming starts on Tumblr. Owners caption their pets' faces with what they did, and dog expressions start being read as human emotions.

  3. 2013
    The big one

    Doge takes over

    Kabosu the Shiba Inu becomes Doge, Know Your Meme's top meme of the year. Later that year Dogecoin launches, and dog faces and crypto are tied together for good.

  4. 2010s
    The tongue

    Blep and Tongue Out Tuesday

    Pet accounts make the little tongue poke-out its own genre. A dog with its tongue showing reads as silly, relaxed and up to something.

  5. Late 2010s
    The wink

    The puppy shows up

    This close-up of a tan puppy, one eye shut and tongue out, starts circulating with no credited owner. It lands on Imgflip as the "Winking puppy" template and spreads through Pinterest boards and group chats.

  6. 2020s
    Reaction image

    The universal ;P

    The wink becomes shorthand for "I know what you did," "trust me" and "just kidding." It gets used as a profile picture, a reply sticker and a TikTok trend of people spotting their own dog's wink.
