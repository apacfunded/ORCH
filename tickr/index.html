FAQ

Questions

What does "paired with a stock" mean?

Your coin's bonding curve and pool are priced in a tokenized stock instead of SOL. If the stock goes up, your coin is worth more in dollars too — on top of its own moves.

Are these real shares?

No. Coins on Tickr are memecoins. They trade against tokenized stocks, but they don't give you ownership of any company.

Do I need the stock to buy a coin?

No. Pay with SOL and Tickr routes it through the stock into the coin in one transaction.

How do holder rewards work?

0.5% of every trade is collected in the paired stock and paid to holders by bag size every 20 minutes. Hold an NVDAx coin, get paid in NVDAx.

Who can use Tickr?

Tokenized stocks aren't available to US persons or in some other regions, so trading on Tickr isn't either. Check your local rules.
