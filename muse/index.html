FAQ

Questions

What does my coin actually get?+

Its own AI creator: a look, a voice, a personality and a language you pick, posting the kinds of videos you chose about your coin.

Is it a real pump.fun coin?+

Yes. Muse builds the standard pump.fun create transaction and you sign it in your own wallet. The coin shows up on pump.fun right away, with you as the dev.

Does Muse hold my keys or funds?+

No. A fresh mint key is made in your browser and your wallet signs. The server only builds and sends the transaction you approved.

What does it cost?+

pump.fun's normal create fee, network fees and any dev buy you choose. Your wallet shows the exact total before you sign.

What is $MUSE?+

The Muse launchpad coin. Its contract address is posted at the top of this page and in the $MUSE section — trust only those.
