    Questions

    Where does the SOL come from?

    Creator fees from every $AFK trade. Every 20 minutes, the pot gets split between everyone who's AFK and sent straight to their wallets.

    How big is my cut?

    Your bag size times your level. If two people hold the same bag, a Legend (5x) gets five times more than someone Idle (1x).

    What resets my timer?

    Selling any $AFK or sending any out of your wallet. Moving to a new wallet counts as selling, so the new wallet starts at Idle.

    Do I have to claim anything?

    No. Just hold. Payouts land in your wallet automatically every 20 minutes.

    Who doesn't get paid?

    The dev wallet, the bonding curve, the liquidity pool and exchange wallets. Bags under the minimum set at launch don't get paid either.
