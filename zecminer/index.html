FAQ

Questions

Do I need to do anything to get paid?+

Hold $ZECMINER and link a Zcash address once. Payouts go out every day at 00:00 UTC, split by how much you hold at snapshot time.

Why Zcash?+

ZEC is mined with Equihash, which has a deep rental market, and it's one of the few big coins built around privacy. Your payouts can arrive fully shielded.

Is there a minimum bag?+

Payouts below the network's dust limit roll over to the next day instead of getting lost, so small holders still collect over time.

Can I check the mining is real?+

Yes. The mining wallet, payout wallet and pool worker page are all public once we launch, and every rental is posted on X.

What if I don't have a Zcash wallet?+

Grab any wallet that supports shielded addresses, copy your receive address, and paste it above. It takes about a minute.
