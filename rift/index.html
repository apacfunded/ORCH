FAQ

Quick answers

Does more supply make my coin worth more?

No. Your coin starts at the same market cap either way. Supply only changes the price of one coin and how many coins a buyer gets.

What can I pick?

Any whole number from 1 million to 1 trillion. Tap a quick pick or type your own.

Can I change it later?

No. Supply is set once, at launch. Nobody can make more coins after that, not even you.

Why pick fewer coins?

Each coin costs more and the number is easy to count. It makes a coin feel rare.

Why pick more coins?

Each coin is very cheap, so a small buy gets a huge bag. Great for classic meme coins.
