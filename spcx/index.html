FAQ

Is this a SpaceX share?

No. $SPCX is a memecoin on Solana. It shares the ticker of SpaceX's Nasdaq stock, but holding it gives you no ownership in SpaceX.

Does Elon get the creator fees?

Yes, that's the plan. They go into the Cybercab Fund. If he verifies a wallet publicly, the full balance gets sent to him. Until then the $SPCX team holds it and won't spend it.

What if Elon never claims it?

We post a claim deadline on X. If the fund is still unclaimed after it, the creator fees go toward buying a flying Tesla Roadster and tokenizing it on Solana.

Is Elon or SpaceX involved?

No. This is a community coin. Neither Elon Musk nor SpaceX is affiliated with it or has endorsed it.

How do I know I have the real CA?

The real contract address starts with SPCX. Copy it from this page or the pinned post on our X.

Where can I check the fund?

The fund wallet goes up at launch. Paste it into Solscan to see every fee that came in.
