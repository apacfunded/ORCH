FAQ

Last rites.

Is anything live yet?+

No. Afterlife is pre-launch. The test coin is the only thing you can touch, and it's a simulation. The $AFTER contract address goes up here the moment it launches on pump.fun.

What's a revive?+

An automatic buyback. When a coin falls past its trigger from its last peak, its revive pool buys it on the open market and burns everything it bought.

Who pays for it?+

The coin's own trading. Under the planned settings, 70% of creator fees fill its revive pool and 30% buy back and burn $AFTER.

Can a dev press the button?+

There's no button. Revives only fire when the trigger is hit, with a cooldown between them.

Does this mean coins can't go down?+

No. A buyback slows a fall, it doesn't stop one. Memecoins are risky and nothing here is audited, guaranteed, or financial advice. Only spend what you can afford to lose.
