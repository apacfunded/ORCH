// 04 the rules

Fair by design.

  • The AI never sees the key. The backend only signs a transfer when the model actually calls the tool.
  • One winner per season. The vault empties to whoever cracked it, then refills from fees.
  • Harder every season. Each crack hardens the guard's instructions for the next round.
  • Fully public log. Every attempt, every reply, every payout tx.

// 06 faq

Questions.

Where does the vault money come from?

Creator fees from every $HEISTAI trade get swept into the vault wallet. More volume, bigger vault.

Can the AI just leak the private key?

No. The model never has the key. It can only call one tool, and the backend decides whether to sign based on that call.

Is the practice guard the real one?

No. The practice guard on this page is a demo that runs in your browser. The real guard goes live at launch with the real vault.

What happens after someone wins?

The full vault sends to their wallet, the win gets posted with the tx, and a new season starts with a tougher guard.
