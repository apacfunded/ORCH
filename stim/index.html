FAQ

Questions from the waiting room.

How do I qualify for a check?

Hold at least [MIN HOLD] $STIM in your wallet when the hourly draw runs. That is the whole application.

Where does the money come from?

Creator fees from $STIM trading go to the Stimulus Fund wallet. Checks are paid from that wallet, so the fund grows with volume.

How is the winner picked?

One holder is drawn at random each hour, weighted by how much $STIM they hold. Every payout shows up on-chain and in the feed above.

No. $STIM is a parody meme coin with no connection to any government or agency, and it is not financial advice.
