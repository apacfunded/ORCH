Questions

Does every coin really get a game?

Yes. Every coin launched on Blox gets its own Roblox game built around its name, ticker and the style you pick. The game is linked on the coin's page.

Where does the game money go?

Revenue from a coin's game is used to buy back that coin and burn it. Each burn is listed on this site with its transaction.

Are the top Roblox games part of Blox?

No. The chart shows the most played games on Roblox so you can pick a style. Those games belong to their own creators and have nothing to do with Blox or any coin.

Is Blox made by Roblox?

No. Blox is an independent project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Roblox Corporation.
