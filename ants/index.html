    FAQ

    Are the ants real trades?

    Yes, once $ANTS is live. Each ant is one on-chain buy and each ant that runs off is a sell. The preview buttons only show on your own screen.

    What happens to the creator fees?

    They get burned. Creator fees are used to buy $ANTS and send it to the burn address, so the supply only goes down.

    What makes an ant bigger?

    Buy size. Small buys are scout ants, mid buys are workers, and big buys are soldier ants.

    What happens when someone sells?

    One ant grabs a piece of food, runs up the tunnel and leaves the colony with a "paper hands" tag.

    What is the queen?

    She lives in the bottom chamber. When $ANTS finishes its bonding curve on pump.fun, the queen gets crowned.
