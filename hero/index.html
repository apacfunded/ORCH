FAQ

Questions

What does the KOL actually get?+

SOL. Your coin’s pump.fun creator fees go to them on every trade. The more your coin trades, the more they earn.

Does the KOL need to sign up first?+

No. You can pick any X handle. Their fees are held for that handle until they sign in with X and claim.

What if they never claim?+

Their fees stay held for them. Unclaimed totals show on the leaderboard so the community can tag them.

Do holders know where the fees go?+

Yes. Every coin on Hero shows which KOL it pays and what share, right on the page.

Is this endorsed by the KOL?+

Not unless they say so. Picking a handle sends them fees. It doesn't mean they back or promote the coin.

Do I need a new coin?+

No. Hero works with coins already on pump.fun. Paste the CA and sign with the wallet that created it.
