Questions

Is Coinfax free?

Yes. Every report is free and there is no sign-up. Paste a contract address and the report prints.

Where does the data come from?

On-chain checks come from RugCheck and market data comes from DexScreener. Both are pulled live when you run the report, so the numbers can change minute to minute.

Does CLEAN TITLE mean a coin is safe?

No. A clean report means none of our checks found a major red flag. Coins can still dump, and devs can still sell. Treat the grade as one input and never as financial advice.

Which coins can I fax?

Any Solana token, including pump.fun coins still on the bonding curve and coins that already migrated.

Why does a report say "partial"?

If the on-chain scanner is busy or rate-limited, we still print the market side of the report and check mint and freeze authority straight from Solana. Run it again in a minute for the full scan.
