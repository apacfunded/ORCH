Questions

What happens to creator fees?

All $FAX creator fees buy back $FAX on the market and send it to a burn address. The tokens are gone for good, so every trade shrinks the supply.

Is Coinfax free?

Yes. Every report is free with no sign-up. Paste a contract address and the report prints.

How do you find bundlers?

We check bundle data from trench.bot first. If that is not available, we read the coin's launch trades and count different wallets that bought in the same block as the launch, plus the share of supply they took. Launch trades can only be read for coins under a day old with normal activity.

Where does the rest of the data come from?

On-chain checks come from RugCheck, market data from DexScreener, and new pairs and launch trades from GeckoTerminal. Everything is pulled live when you open a report.

Does LOW RISK mean a coin is safe?

No. It means none of our checks found a major red flag. Coins can still dump and devs can still sell. Use the score as one input, never as financial advice.

Why does a report say "partial"?

If a data source is busy, we still print what we have and check mint and freeze straight from Solana. Run it again in a minute for the full scan.
