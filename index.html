FAQ

Questions people ask

Does the person I name need a wallet?

No. Their fees wait under their handle. When they want the money, they sign in with that account and it goes to Apple Pay in dollars.

What does "paired with $PAID" mean?

Orchid is built together with the team behind $PAID, the memecoin. Both coins push the same idea: trading should pay real people.

How is $ORCHID launching?

Fair launch on pump.fun. No presale, so everyone buys on the same curve at the same time.

Can the fee split change later?

No. The split locks the moment the coin launches, so the person you named always gets their share.

What is $PAID?

$PAID is the memecoin behind Paid, a Solana app that routes creator fees to real X accounts. It has paid out over $1.4M so far. Orchid is built with the Paid team and paired with $PAID.

How do I know a coin is the real $ORCHID?

Only trust the contract address posted on this site and on @Orchid_Pad. Anything else using the old Orchard name isn't us.
