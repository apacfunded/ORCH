FAQ

Does every coin really get a game?

Yes. Every coin launched on RobloxPad gets its own Roblox game built around its name, ticker and theme, linked on the coin's page.

Where does the game money go?

Revenue from the game is used to buy back that game's coin and burn it. Every burn shows up in the burn log with its transaction.

Is RobloxPad made by Roblox?

No. RobloxPad is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Roblox Corporation.
