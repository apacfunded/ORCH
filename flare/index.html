FAQ

Good questions.

What is Flare?

Flare is a Solana launchpad where coins pay for their own marketing. Part of every coin's creator fees automatically buys DexScreener boosts, trending spots and ads for that coin.

Can the dev pull the hype money?

No. The hype share goes into a wallet that can only spend on marketing for that coin. Nobody can withdraw it, the dev included.

What does $FLARE do?

15% of creator fees from every coin launched on Flare buys back and burns $FLARE. More coins and more volume mean bigger burns.

How do I know the boosts really happened?

Every flare shows up on the live log with the coin, the channel, the SOL spent and the transaction, so anyone can check it.

When does a flare fire?

As soon as a coin's hype wallet has enough SOL for the next boost tier or trending spot. Busy coins fire more often.

Is it on pump.fun?

Yes. Coins launch through pump.fun and trade like any other pump.fun coin. Flare handles the fee routing and the marketing buys.
