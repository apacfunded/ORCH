FAQ

Questions

Who gets the profits?

You do. Creator fees from your token go to you, minus a share that buys and burns $STUDIO and a share that keeps the AI running. The exact split is set at launch and shown on every token.

Can it make just a script?

Yes. Switch to Script and ask for one feature, like a double jump, a shop GUI or a day and night cycle. You get the Luau plus a Studio file with it already in place.

Do I need Roblox Studio?

Yes. The builder gives you a place file. Roblox Studio is free on Windows and Mac and opens the file directly.

Does it publish my game or launch my token for me?

No. You publish the game from your own Roblox account and launch the token from your own wallet on pump.fun. $STUDIO prepares everything so each step is a paste and a click.

What can it build?

Obbies, tycoons, simulators, tower defense, racing and hide and seek maps with working scripts. Bigger games come out as a solid starting point you keep editing in Studio.

Is it free to use?

Each build runs on your own Claude account and uses your Claude usage. You'll be asked to allow it the first time.

Is $STUDIO an investment?

No. It's a memecoin. Prices can go to zero, so only buy what you can afford to lose.
