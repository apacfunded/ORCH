How it works

  1. Connect a wallet

    Sign in with Phantom, Solflare or Backpack. Signing in is a free message, not a transaction. You start with 10 play SOL.

  2. Back a side

    Every coin runs Up/Down rounds from 5 minutes to a day, a daily price ladder and head-to-heads. Your multiplier moves as the pool fills.

  3. Get paid on the chart

    At close we read the coin's market cap and settle. Winners split the losing side, less 3% of winnings. One-sided rounds refund.

Is this real money?

Not yet. Tally runs on play SOL while the on-chain pools are built. Your wins, losses and leaderboard rank are real; the SOL isn't.

Where do prices come from?

DexScreener, using each coin's most liquid pair. We record market cap every 20 seconds and settle on the reading closest to close.

Why can't I bet right before close?

Betting locks 1 minute before a short round ends, and 5% of the round for longer ones, so nobody can bet once the answer is obvious.

Which coins are listed?

Big Pump.fun coins with steady on-chain trading. Short rounds only run on the busiest ones, and the limits get much stricter before any real SOL is involved.

How is the payout worked out?

Winners get their stake back plus a share of the losing pool equal to their share of the winning pool. The 3% fee comes out of winnings only, never the stake.
