The loop

How PokePacks works

Fees fill the pot

Every buy and sell sends creator fees to the public rip wallet.

The pot rips a pack

At 1 SOL, the bot opens a mystery pack on pump.fun. Bigger pots open better packs.

A holder gets the slab

The pulled card goes to a random holder, weighted by bag size. Hold 0.1% or more to qualify.

Grails feed the chart

When a slab is worth more than the pack cost, part of the difference buys back and burns $POKEPACKS.

FAQ

Questions

Are the cards real?

Yes. Pack pulls are tokenized cards backed by real graded slabs held in an insured vault. Winners can hold them, sell them or redeem the physical slab.

How do I qualify for pulls?

Hold at least 0.1% of supply when a pack rips. More $POKEPACKS means better odds of being picked, but every qualifying wallet has a chance.

Can I see the pack contents before a rip?

Yes. Each pack on the shelf above lists its full card pool and tier odds. The pool shown here is example data until launch, when it switches to the live pump.fun pack lists.

Where can I check the fee wallet?

The rip wallet address will be posted here and on X. Every pack purchase and slab transfer can be checked on Solscan.
