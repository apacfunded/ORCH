    FAQ

    Questions

    What is Rakeback?

    A launchpad for pump.fun coins. It charges a 1% fee on every trade and sends the whole fee to people who hold $RAKE.

    Who gets the 1% fee?

    $RAKE holders get all of it, split by how much each wallet holds. The team does not take a cut first.

    How does the team make money?

    By holding $RAKE like everyone else. The team wallet receives its share of each payout and nothing more.

    Do I need to stake or claim?

    No. Keep $RAKE in your wallet and payouts arrive in SOL on their own.

    What do creators get?

    Creators launch for free, keep their own pump.fun creator fees and get listed on the Rakeback board.

    Where is the $RAKE contract address?

    $RAKE has not launched yet. The CA will be posted on this page when it does. Any coin using the name before then is not ours.
