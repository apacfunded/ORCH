    FAQ

    Questions

    What does "decompile" mean?

    Games ship as compiled machine code. Decompiling turns that back into readable code, so the AI can see how movement, combat, building and the rest actually work.

    Why one shared language?

    Different games run on different engines and languages, so they can't talk to each other. Splice translates both into Splice-IR, one common language, so systems from each game can run side by side in one build.

    How do I vote?

    Hold $SPLICE and connect your wallet when voting opens. Your vote weight matches your holdings at the snapshot.

    Who pays for the AI?

    Creator fees. 70% goes to the splice pool that funds compute, and 30% buys back $SPLICE.

    Is the Splice Lab demo real?

    The lab shows how a splice run works step by step. Real splices are picked by the holder vote and funded by the fee pool.
