How it works

Fair from the first block

1

Coin goes live

The full supply sits on a bonding curve with virtual liquidity, like pump.fun. Real buys fill it. Minting is switched off forever.

2

Nobody knows when

Trading opens 2 to 14 seconds after launch, at a slot picked from a block hash that didn't exist yet. Buys packed into the launch block fail.

3

Early costs more

Buying in the first minute pays a sniper tax. Selling early-bought tokens in the first 15 minutes pays an early-sell tax. Both go to liquidity.

4

Everyone can see

Each coin page shows wallets that look like one person and how much they hold, so buyers can decide with the facts.

What is bundling?

One person buys a new coin from many wallets at once, often in the same block as the launch, so they get a big share at the lowest price. Then they sell into everyone who buys after them.

How does the random open slot work?

When a coin launches, the program waits for a block that hasn't been made yet and uses its hash to pick the opening slot. Nobody can know it when they build the launch transaction, so buys bundled with the launch are rejected.

What's the sniper tax?

On the opening slot, buys pay a 90% fee. It falls steadily to the normal 1% over about a minute. The extra goes into the coin's own tax pool, which becomes liquidity when the coin graduates.

What's the early-sell tax?

For 15 minutes after trading opens, selling tokens bought in the first 2 minutes pays an extra tax. It starts at 50% and falls to 0. Tokens your wallet bought after the first 2 minutes are "clean" and sell with only the normal fee.

Can bundlers dodge the sell tax by moving tokens?

No. Only tokens your own wallet bought after the first 2 minutes are clean. Tokens sent in from another wallet are never clean, so moving them changes nothing.

Is there a wallet limit?

For the first 5 minutes, no wallet can hold more than 2% of the supply, and only 4 buys can land in the same slot. The trade box shows the biggest buy you're allowed right now.

Can the creator buy at launch?

Yes, up to 2% of the supply, at the normal fee. It's marked as a dev buy on the coin page so everyone knows.

When does a coin graduate?

When its curve sells out, about 85 SOL of real buys. Its pool on a DEX is created once the early-sell tax is over, so early buyers can't dump tax-free somewhere else.

Can a bundler still get through?

Partly. Someone patient with many wallets can still buy after the opening. Our tests show the rules cut their best-case profit by roughly two thirds, and the bundle check shows the linked wallets on the coin page.

What does the bundle score mean?

0 to 100. It goes up with the share of supply held or already dumped by linked wallets, links to the creator, and how many early buyers used brand-new wallets. Any group of 5 or more linked wallets is at least Medium.

Terms and risks

Read this before you use Xray

Last updated October 20, 2026.

Nothing here is financial advice

Xray is a tool for launching and trading coins. Nothing on this site is investment, financial, legal or tax advice. Only spend what you can afford to lose.

Memecoins are extremely risky

Most coins lose most or all of their value. The launch rules make bundling harder and more visible. They don't make any coin safe, and a clean bundle score is not a recommendation.

The bundle check is an estimate

It links wallets from public on-chain data. It can miss wallets that hide their links, and it can link wallets that aren't related. Treat it as one signal.

Your wallet, your responsibility

Xray never holds your coins and never asks for your seed phrase. Connecting a wallet only shares your public address. We will never DM you first.
