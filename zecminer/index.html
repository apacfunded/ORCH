Questions

How do I get paid?

Hold $ZECMINER. Each block the bot finds goes into the holder pool, split by bag size and sent on the next payout cycle.

Why Zcash?

Zcash is still proof of work. Blocks land about every 75 seconds on Equihash, so the rig finds rewards all day.

Do I need to claim anything?

No. Buy and hold. Payouts go out on a timer with nothing to claim or stake.

What makes the rig faster?

Volume. More trades means more creator fees, which rent more hashpower and find more blocks.
