Arizona Man's Cat Becomes First Feline to Fly With SpaceX

A Surprise resident says his cat, Biscuit, is now the most well-traveled pet in the state after hitching a ride on a SpaceX launch.

By Dana Whitfield, Science Correspondent · Updated 7:42 PM MST

Jake, a lifelong space fan from Surprise, Arizona, has done what no pet owner before him has managed: he sent his cat to space with SpaceX. The cat, a black-and-white shorthair named Biscuit, completed the trip and is reportedly in excellent health.

According to Jake, the idea started as a joke between friends and "got out of hand very quickly." After weeks of paperwork, a custom harness and what he described as "a lot of treats," Biscuit was cleared for flight.

"Honestly, the cat handled it better than I would have."

Neighbors say Biscuit has been unbothered since returning home, spending most of the day asleep in a sunbeam. Jake says he's already fielding requests from friends who want their pets on the next flight.

Space historians note the feat follows in the pawprints of Félicette, the French cat who flew to the edge of space in 1963. Jake says Biscuit will be celebrating with "a full can of tuna, the good kind."
