    FAQ

    Common questions.

    Do I have to claim anything?

    No. Just hold. LTC is sent to your wallet automatically every day at 00:00 UTC.

    How much will I get?

    Your share of each day's LTC equals your share of the supply. Hold 1% of supply and you get 1% of the holder payout. Use the estimate above to see rough numbers.

    How do I get Litecoin in a Solana wallet?

    You receive wrapped LTC on Solana, sent to the same wallet that holds $LTCMINER. You can swap it or bridge it to the Litecoin network anytime.

    What is the AI actually doing?

    Mining Litecoin means guessing numbers until one produces a hash below the network's target. The AI runs the rented machines, picks where to search, and keeps them guessing 24/7. Each rental and each payout is posted with its transaction.

    Will payouts always be the same?

    No. More trading means more fees and more mining. Payouts also drop when Litecoin mining gets harder.
