How it works

Three steps to fair.

Your coin launches on pump.fun. Fairway makes the start fair and shows everyone that it was.

1

Launch on pump.fun

Fill in your coin and launch it from your own wallet. It goes live on pump.fun and anyone can trade it right away.

2

Fair rules from the start

The whole supply sits on the curve. No presale and no team bag. The dev can buy 2 SOL at most, and that buy is locked automatically, so the dev can't dump on you.

3

Buyers see it was fair

Every coin gets a public fair report. If the early buyers are separate people it keeps its Fair badge. If one person bought from lots of wallets, it gets flagged.
