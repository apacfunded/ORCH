    FAQ

    Questions

    Do I need to sign up to get paid?+

    No. Hold $BTCMINER in your wallet. At 00:00 UTC every day we snapshot holders and send out that day's mined BTC, split by bag size.

    Is the mining real?+

    Yes. Fees rent SHA-256 hashpower that points at a public Bitcoin pool. The mining wallet, pool worker page and every rental are posted, so you can check the numbers yourself.

    How much will I get?+

    It depends on trading volume, Bitcoin's difficulty and how much you hold. More volume means more hashpower, which means more sats for everyone.

    What about tiny bags?+

    Payouts too small to send roll over to the next day, so small holders still collect over time.

    Where do I follow updates?+

    Payout posts, rental receipts and mining updates go up on X first.
