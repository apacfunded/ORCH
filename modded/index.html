How it works

One coin, one mod.

Every launch is a mod request with money behind it. The coin pays for its own mod.

01 / Launch

Launch a coin for a mod

Pick a game, describe the mod, and give it a name and ticker.

02 / Fund

Fees fund the build

The coin's creator fees pay AI to build the mod. More volume, faster build.

03 / Build

Watch it get built

Each coin has a build page with live progress, code updates and screenshots.

04 / Burn

Mod drops, coin burns

The mod is free to download. After release, fees buy back and burn the coin.

Rules

Mods, not cheats.

Modded builds mods for games you play on your own or with friends. Anything that gives an edge in online matches gets blocked at launch.

FAQ

Questions

What happens when I launch a coin?

Your coin goes live on pump.fun with its own build page. Its creator fees go toward building the mod you described.

Who gets the mod?

Everyone. Finished mods are free to download from the coin's build page.

What happens to fees after the mod ships?

After release, the coin's fees buy back that coin and burn it.

What games can I launch for?

Single-player and mod-friendly games. Mods for competitive online games are blocked so nothing turns into a cheat.
