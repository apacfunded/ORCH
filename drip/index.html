How it works

Three steps. One approval.

1

Fill in your order

How much SOL, which token, and over how long.

2

Approve once

Your SOL moves into a fresh order wallet made just for this buy.

3

Drip does the rest

It buys a little every minute, then sends all your tokens back to your wallet.

Why buy slowly?

A big buy pushes the price up before it fills. Small buys don't.

Questions

Is my main wallet safe?

Yes. Drip never signs anything from your main wallet. It only uses the SOL you approve into the order wallet, and its key stays in your browser.

Do I have to keep the page open?

Yes, buys are sent from this page. If you close it, the order pauses and you can resume when you come back on the same device.

Which tokens work?

Any Solana token with a SOL route on Jupiter, plus pump.fun coins still on the bonding curve.

What does it cost?

Drip is free. You only pay normal Solana network fees, capped at 0.0001 SOL per buy.

What is $DRIP?

The token behind Drip. More features for holders are coming.
