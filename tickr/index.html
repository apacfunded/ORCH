FAQ

Questions

What's a house coin?

One of Tickr's four memecoins — $tSPY, $tNVDA, $tTSLA and $tXAI. Each one trades against a real tokenized stock. They're memecoins, not shares, and don't give you ownership of any company.

Why does every coin have to pick a team?

Your coin is priced in its house coin. Every buy of your coin is also a buy of the house coin, so teams grow together — and you get a stock's momentum under your meme.

Do I need the stock or the house coin to buy?

No. Pay with SOL and Tickr routes it through the stock and the house coin into your coin in one transaction.

Where do fees go?

0.5% of every trade buys back and burns the house coin your coin is paired with. 0.3% goes to the coin's creator, 0.2% to the platform.

Who can use Tickr?

Tokenized stocks aren't available to US persons or in some other regions, so trading on Tickr isn't either. Check your local rules.
