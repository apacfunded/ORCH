Questions

What does "graduate" mean?

New pump.fun coins trade on a bonding curve. Once a coin reaches a set market cap, it graduates: it leaves the curve and moves to a regular liquidity pool.

Should I pick a low or high cap?

A low cap gets you off the curve quickly. A high cap keeps the coin on the curve longer, which gives early buyers more room before it migrates. There's no right answer, it depends on your coin.

Can I change the cap after launch?

No. It's locked at launch, so holders always know where the finish line is.

Is my coin still on pump.fun?

Yes. Arc launches through pump.fun, so your coin has its normal pump.fun page and chart.

What is $ARC?

$ARC is the official Arc token. It was the first coin ever launched on Arc.
