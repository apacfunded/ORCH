FAQ

Does my coin still launch on pump.fun?

Yes. It launches on pump.fun like normal. Cuffed just sends your dev buy into a lock instead of your wallet.

Can I unlock early?

No. That's the point. Once it's cuffed, nobody can unlock it early, not even us.

What's the difference between the two unlock types?

"A little every day" frees an equal slice daily until the lock ends. "All at the end" keeps everything locked until the last day, then frees it all at once.

Can I still earn creator fees?

Yes. Creator fees go to you as normal. Only the tokens from your dev buy are locked.

What is $CUFFED?

The coin behind the launchpad. Its own dev buy is cuffed too.
