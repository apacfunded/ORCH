FAQ

Questions

Where does the data come from?

Straight from the Solana blockchain. Mirror reads each tracked wallet's recent transactions and pulls coin names and prices from DexScreener.

Does Mirror trade for me?

No. Mirror never touches your wallet. The Mirror button opens Jupiter with the coin loaded, and you confirm the buy yourself.

Can I track my own list?

Yes. Add any wallet in the live feed. It's saved in your browser, so it's still there next time.

What does $MIRROR do?

Creator fees pay for faster data and new tracked wallets. Holders get early access to instant alerts when a tracked trader buys.

Is copying trades safe?

Top traders lose trades too, and by the time you mirror, the price may have moved. Only trade what you can afford to lose. Nothing here is financial advice.
