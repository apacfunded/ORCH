    How it works

    Three steps, all in the open.

    1. 01

      Fees fill the pot

      Every $RBXR trade sends creator fees to the Reserve wallet.

    2. 02

      The Reserve buys Limiteds

      When the pot is full, it buys a Limited at its Rolimons value and locks it in the Reserve.

    3. 03

      Holders get Robux

      Every hour one holder is picked, weighted by bag size, and paid in Robux to their Roblox account.

    FAQ

    How do I get paid?

    Hold at least [MIN HOLD] $RBXR and link your Roblox username to your wallet on this site. If you're picked in the hourly draw, Robux goes to that account.

    Where do the Limiteds come from?

    The Reserve buys them with creator fees once the pot hits the next buy amount. Every buy shows up in the activity feed with its Rolimons value.

    Can I see what the Reserve holds?

    Yes. Top holdings and every buy and payout are listed above, and the Reserve wallet is public on-chain.

    Is this connected to Roblox?
