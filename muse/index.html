FAQ

What does my coin actually get?

Its own AI TikTok creator: a look based on your coin image, a voice, a personality from the vibe you pick, and an account that posts short videos about your coin.

Is it a real pump.fun coin?

Yes. Muse builds the standard pump.fun create transaction and you sign it in your own wallet. The coin shows up on pump.fun right away, with you as the dev.

Does Muse hold my keys or funds?

No. A fresh mint key is made in your browser, and your wallet signs. The server only builds the transaction.

How often does the influencer post?

Every day once it's live. Posts talk about your coin, its chart and its community, in the vibe you picked.

What is $MUSE?

The Muse launchpad coin. The contract address goes up here at launch — anything posted before that isn't us.
