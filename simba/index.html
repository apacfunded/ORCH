Meet Simba

Simba is a spotted Bengal with a silver chain, a scratching-post throne and zero interest in following rules.

He climbs to the top of the kitchen cabinets because every king needs a high throne. But Simba knows not every cat gets a warm house and a full bowl. Thousands of cats are waiting in shelters right now for food, vet care and a family.

So $SIMBA is a memecoin with one job: 100% of the creator fees go to cat shelters. Every buy and every sell sends a little more to cats who need it. Simba keeps the chain. The shelters get the fees.

Our partner

$SIMBA x Greater Good Charities

Greater Good Charities is a nonprofit that works across people, animals and the environment. It listens to communities and moves fast, getting aid, expertise and funding to where it's needed most, during disasters and long after.

Their Care Packages for Cats program supplies shelters with what cats need every day: food, litter, medicine and comfort.

FAQ

Do all the creator fees really go to shelters?

Yes. 100% of $SIMBA creator fees go to cat shelters and rescues. The donation wallet is public and every donation is posted with its receipt and transaction.

Which shelters get the money?

Cat shelters and rescues that need food, litter and vet care. Donations go through Greater Good Charities' Care Packages for Cats program, which supplies shelters with food, litter and vet care. Our first goal is $5,000.

Is Simba a real cat?

Yes. Simba is a real Bengal who lives indoors, wears his chain and climbs everything. The spotted Bengal photos are him. The shelter cats are the ones we're helping.

Is this an investment?

No. $SIMBA is a memecoin for fun and for a good cause. It has no promise of value. Only use money you can afford to lose.
