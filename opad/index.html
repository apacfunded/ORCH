Questions.

What is OPAD?

The front door for Optimism coins. You set up your coin on OPAD and it launches on pump.fun's Optimism support.

Do I need ETH or a new wallet?

No. You launch through pump.fun and pay in SOL.

Where does my coin trade?

On pump.fun, under Optimism. OPAD lists it on the board and links straight to it.

What happens at graduation?

The coin graduates on pump.fun when its bonding curve fills, same as any pump.fun coin.

Where do the fees go?

pump.fun's creator fees go to the coin's creator. OPAD's own fees: 50% to creators, 30% buys back $OPAD, 20% to the OPAD treasury.

What is $OPAD?

OPAD's own coin, launched on pump.fun. OPAD fees buy it back.
