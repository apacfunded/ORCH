What's a mod

Add anything to a game you already own.

A mod adds new items, powers or ways to play. Normally it takes a programmer and a weekend. With Modded it takes one sentence.

New items

A coal-powered jetpack, a backpack with no limit, a grappling hook.

New powers

A spell that rains gold, a shout that calls a storm, double jump.

New ways to play

Boss rush, a lava-rising round, a drift race in story mode.

FAQ

Questions

Anything else, ask on X.

Is this real?

Yes. The build console up top and the mod cards are examples until launch. Real mods are built with each game's own modding tools once their coin hits its goal, and every mod is tested before it can be downloaded.

Which games does it support?

Games that allow mods: Minecraft, Skyrim, GTA V story mode, Stardew Valley, Terraria and your own Roblox games. Black Ops 2, Black Ops 1, Modern Warfare and Skate are next. If a game doesn't allow mods, Modded can't mod it.

Will it build cheats or aimbots?

No. Modded only builds single-player mods, co-op mods for friends and private-server content. Online cheats break games' rules and get accounts banned, so Modded refuses those prompts.

How do I install a mod?

See the install guide. It's three steps for every game, and each mod ships with its own guide.

Do I need to launch a coin?

Yes. Every mod starts as a coin, and its creator fees pay for the build, so nobody pays for the mod up front.

Are mods free?

Yes. Every mod is free to download.

What if my coin doesn't hit its goal?

The coin keeps trading. The mod builds the moment it hits its market cap goal, with no deadline.

How does the $MODDED burn work?

After a mod ships, 30% of its coin's creator fees go to a public buyback wallet that market-buys $MODDED and sends it to the burn address. Every burn is public on-chain.
