Features

Built for how memecoins actually trade.

Order splitting

One order becomes up to 360 equal buys, sized automatically from your amount and duration.

Best-route execution

Each buy is quoted on Jupiter. Tokens still on the pump.fun curve fall back to PumpPortal automatically.

Flexible schedule

Choose 10 minutes to 3 hours, and a buy every 30 seconds to 5 minutes.

Pause or end anytime

Pause to wait out a dump, or end early and keep everything that already filled.

Automatic settlement

When the order completes, tokens and unused SOL are sent to your wallet and empty accounts are closed.

Retries built in

A failed buy is retried. If it keeps failing, that SOL is skipped and returned at settlement.

How it works

Four steps from order to settlement.

1

Configure

Connect Phantom, Solflare or Backpack. Enter the token, amount and duration.

2

Approve once

Fund a session wallet created for this order. This is the only signature you make.

3

Execute

Drip sends one buy per interval and logs each transaction with a Solscan link.

4

Settle

Tokens and leftover SOL return to your wallet automatically when the order ends.

Security

Your main wallet never signs a trade.

Drip uses a session wallet that holds only what you fund for the current order.

Scoped funding

Only the SOL you approve moves to the session wallet. Drip has no access to the rest of your wallet.

Key stays on your device

The session key is created and stored in your browser. It's never sent to a server.

Exportable and recoverable

Back up the key or settle to your wallet at any time, even mid-order.

On-chain receipts

Every buy and the final settlement are standard Solana transactions you can verify on Solscan.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is Drip?

Drip is a time-weighted buy tool (TWAP) for Solana. It turns one order into many smaller buys over a period you choose.

Do I need to keep the page open?

Yes. Orders run from your browser. If you close the tab the order pauses, and you can resume it when you return on the same device.

Which tokens are supported?

Any SPL token with a SOL route on Jupiter, plus pump.fun tokens still on the bonding curve.

What does it cost?

Drip charges no fee. You pay standard Solana network and priority fees, capped at 0.0001 SOL per buy. Buys routed through PumpPortal include their routing fee.

What happens if a buy fails?

It's retried after 15 seconds. After three failures that buy is skipped, and its SOL is returned when the order settles.

What is $DRIP?

$DRIP is the token behind Drip. Holder features such as scheduled orders and timed sells are planned next.
