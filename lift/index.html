Questions

Good to know.

So what is a "stock pair"?

Most memecoins are priced in SOL. A Lift coin is priced in a tokenized stock instead. That means its dollar price follows two things: how much people want the coin, and the price of the stock.

Am I buying real shares?

No. Coins on Lift are memecoins. They trade against tokenized stocks, but they don't give you ownership of any company.

Do I need to own the stock to buy a coin?

No. Swap SOL on Jupiter and it routes through the stock into the coin.

What if the stock goes down?

Then the tide goes out: every coin paired with it is worth less in dollars, even if nobody sells. It works in both directions.

How do payouts work?

Reward coins carry a 1% or 3% transfer tax. It's swapped into the paired stock and shared among holders by how much they hold.

Where do coins actually launch?

On stonk.fun, using Raydium LaunchLab. Lift helps you pick a pair and set up your coin, then sends you there to sign the launch with your own wallet.

Who can use Lift?

Tokenized stocks aren't available to US persons or in some other regions, so Lift isn't either. Check your local rules.
