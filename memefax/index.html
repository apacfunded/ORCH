    FAQ

    Is it really free?

    Yes. Every report is free for everyone. $FAX creator fees pay for the site.

    How is the score made?

    It blends bundler share, top holder share, dev holdings, coin age and risk labels into one number out of 100. Higher is safer.

    Is this financial advice?

    No. A fax is a history report, not a promise. Always do your own research.

    How does $FAX work?
