FAQ

What does "graduate" mean?

A new pump.fun coin trades on a bonding curve. When it reaches its target market cap, it graduates and moves to a regular liquidity pool.

Why choose my own graduation cap?

A low cap gets you off the curve fast. A high cap keeps the coin on the curve longer, so early buyers get more room before migration.

Can I change the cap after launch?

No. The target is locked when the coin launches, so holders always know where the finish line is.

Is my coin still on pump.fun?

Yes. Arc launches through pump.fun, so your coin shows up there with its normal page and chart.

What is $ARC?

$ARC is the Arc platform coin, launched on Arc itself.
