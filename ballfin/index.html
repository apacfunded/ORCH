Good to know

Questions

Where does the $100 come from?

From $BALLFIN's own pump.fun creator fees. Every buy and sell pays a small fee into the jackpot. Nothing is taken from holders.

Do bigger bags get better odds?

No. Every eligible wallet gets exactly one marble, so every holder has the same chance each race.

Do I need to claim?

No. Winners are paid in SOL straight to the wallet holding the $BALLFIN.

How is the winner decided?

When the jackpot hits $100, holders are snapshotted and a random seed picks the winning wallet. The race on this page plays out that result, and the snapshot size, seed and payout tx are posted on the Winners wall.

What if I sell?

You need to be holding at the moment of the snapshot. Sell and you're off the track for that race; buy back in for the next one.

How often are races?

As often as trading fills the jackpot. More volume means more $100 races.

What are warm-up laps?

Practice runs so the track is never empty. They use numbered marbles, not wallets, and pay nothing.

Is this connected to GTA VI?

No. Ballfin is a fan memecoin named after the lottery in the game, and isn't affiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two.
