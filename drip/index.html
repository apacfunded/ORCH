How it works

Set it once. Drip does the rest.

Step 1

Connect and set up

Connect Phantom, Solflare or Backpack. Paste the coin and choose the amount and time.

Step 2

Fund your drip wallet

You approve one transfer into a fresh drip wallet made just for this. That's the only popup.

Step 3

Drip buys on a timer

Every interval it sends one small buy through Jupiter, or pump.fun's own pool if Jupiter has no route yet.

Step 4

Tokens come home

When it finishes or you press Stop, all tokens and leftover SOL go back to your wallet automatically.

Safety

What happens to your SOL

Your main wallet is never touched

Drip can't sign anything from your main wallet. It only gets the SOL you send to the drip wallet.

The drip wallet lives in this browser

Its key is stored on your device only. Tap "Show backup key" to save it, or send everything home anytime.

No Drip fee right now

You pay normal Solana network fees. If a buy routes through PumpPortal, they charge their own small fee.

FAQ

Questions

What is Drip in one sentence?

It's DCA for memecoins: instead of one big buy, it makes many small buys over minutes or hours.

Do I need to keep the page open?

Yes, buys are sent from this page. If you close it, your drip pauses. Open the page again on the same device and press Resume.

What if a buy fails?

It retries on the next tick. Any SOL that didn't get spent goes back to your wallet when the drip ends.

Which coins work?

Any Solana token with a SOL route on Jupiter, plus pump.fun coins still on the bonding curve.

What does $DRIP do?

$DRIP is the coin behind the tool. Holder perks like drip-sells and scheduled drips are next on the list.
