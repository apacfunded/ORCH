FAQ

Questions people ask

Does the player need crypto?

No. Their rewards wait under their Roblox username. They prove the account is theirs and pick Robux or a Limited.

How do players prove it's them?

They paste a short code into the About section of their Roblox profile. Once we see it, the rewards unlock.

How are Limiteds valued?

By RAP from Rolimons, the price Roblox traders already use. Players see the value before they pick.

How is $ROPAY launching?

Fair launch on pump.fun. No presale. Only trust the CA posted on this site.

Can the fee split change later?

No. It locks when the coin launches, so the player always gets their share.

Is this made by Roblox?

No. RoPay is a fan project and isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Roblox Corporation.
