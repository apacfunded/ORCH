    FAQ
    Questions
    
      
    
        
    Is this real?
    Yes. The studio on this page is a demo that shows sample mods. Live mods are built with each game's official modding tools once their coin hits the goal, and every mod is tested before anyone can download it.
    
        
    Does it work for any game?
    It works with games that allow mods, like Minecraft, Skyrim, GTA V story mode, Stardew Valley, Terraria, many Unity games and your own Roblox games. If a game doesn't allow mods, Render can't mod it.
    
        
    Will it make cheats or aimbots?
    No. Render only builds single-player mods, co-op mods for friends and private-server content. Cheats for online games break those games' rules and get accounts banned, so Render refuses those prompts.
    
        
    How do I install a mod?
    Check the install guide above. It's three steps for every game, and each mod comes with its own short guide too.
    
        
    Are the mods free?
    Yes. Mods are free to download. The creator earns from the mod coin's trading fees, not from selling the mod.
    
        
    What happens if my coin never hits the goal?
    The coin still trades normally. The mod stays locked until the coin reaches a $10K market cap, with no deadline.
    
        
    How does the $RENDER burn work?
    30% of creator fees from every mod coin go to a public buyback wallet. It market-buys $RENDER and sends it to the burn address. Every burn is public on-chain.
