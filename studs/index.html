How a game gets built

Fees stack up like bricks. Each stage unlocks when a coin's creator fees reach its total.

Stage 1
1 SOL

Game plan. The AI writes the design doc: loop, rules, rewards and map layout.

Stage 2
3 SOL

Map. Terrain, parts and spawn areas built in Roblox Studio.

Stage 3
6 SOL

Scripts. Luau for the core game: checkpoints, currency, shops, leaderboards.

Stage 4
10 SOL

Playtest. Bots play it, the AI fixes what breaks and balances rewards.

Stage 5
15 SOL

Publish. The game goes live on Roblox, linked from its coin page. Fees after that fund updates.

Questions

What is Studs?

A launchpad where every coin is a Roblox game. The coin's creator fees pay an AI that builds the game in stages until it's live on Roblox.

Where do the fees go?

Creator fees from each coin go into that game's build fund. When the fund reaches the next stage's total, the AI builds that stage.

What happens after a game is published?

Fees keep coming in and pay for updates: new levels, items, events and fixes.

Who owns the game?

The game is published under the Studs Roblox group and linked to its coin. Its page shows every change the AI made.

What is $STUDS?

The token behind the launchpad. The contract address will be posted here at launch.
