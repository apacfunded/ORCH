FAQ

Quick answers before you search.

Do seeds work on both Java and Bedrock?

Most seeds since 1.18 share terrain on both, but structures can sit in different places. Pick your edition before you search and results are checked for it.

How accurate are the coordinates?

Coordinates point to the structure's center chunk. Fly or walk there and it will be within a few blocks.

What do I get for holding $SEED?

Unlimited searches, Seed of the Week drops before anyone else, and votes on featured seeds.
