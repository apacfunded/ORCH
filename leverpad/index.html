Questions

Can I open a long today?

Not yet. Coins launch now and their pools fill from trading fees. Longs switch on once the long engine is finished and audited, and the site will say so here.

Who controls a coin's pool?

Each pool is a separate wallet that Lever's server controls. It's set as the coin's pump.fun creator, so creator fees go there, not to the launcher. Pools only pay winning longs.

Can someone pump a coin to fake a win?

Longs will settle on a 5-minute average price with a minimum hold time, so a quick buy can't fake a win.

Do liquidations dump the coin?

No. Longs are settled from the pool, so nothing is sold on-chain when someone is liquidated.

Can a dev long their own coin?

No. The wallet that launched a coin will be blocked from longing it.
