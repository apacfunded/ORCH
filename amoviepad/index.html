    FAQ

    Who makes the movie?

    The AI does everything. It writes the script, plans the shots, writes every video prompt and edits the final cut. The creator only picks the title, genre and runtime.

    When does the next clip get made?

    Every 0.1 SOL your coin earns in pump.fun creator fees generates the next clip (about 8 seconds of video). The site checks every few minutes and logs each new clip in the Director's Log.

    Can I give it an idea?

    Yes, optionally. Drop a one-line idea at launch and the AI builds the story around it. Leave it blank and it writes the story from your title and genre.

    How long will my movie be?

    Exactly the runtime you pick at launch, from 1 minute up to 90. The AI writes the script to fit, so a 3-minute movie is about 23 shots.

    What happens when it's finished?

    The full cut premieres on the coin's page and moves to the Premiered tab. The movie stays on A Movie Pad for good.

    Where do coins launch?

    On pump.fun. A Movie Pad runs the AI director and the film budget on top of it.
